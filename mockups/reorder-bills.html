Two directions to compare. Placeholder data; the month is Oct 2026.

Both: ↑ ↓ move a bill within its group, or a whole group up or down. Each tap saves straight away in one step, so there's no Save. If saving fails, the row moves back and a message says so. The first row's ↑ and the last row's ↓ are greyed out. Moving a bill to another group stays in the bill sheet (Group menu).

A · In the group sheet Tap ✎ on a group header. The sheet gets an “Order” list for that group's bills and a “Group position” row. The Bills card itself doesn't change.
