Saving 3 of 4…Saving…

Save writes the payslip, its lines and one received income entry for the net amount, dated the check date, in a single transaction. On Home that entry replaces the estimated paycheck, and 401(k) match / HSA show under Savings.

Duplicates (same job, check date and net as a saved payslip) are flagged and never saved twice.

Skipped uploads: a payday that has passed keeps its estimated paycheck as received income (marked estimated) until a payslip replaces it, so months stay right when you only upload payslips that differ.

Later: AI reading for unknown layouts and scans (the AI step), and tapping a saved payslip to view or delete it.
