Placeholder data. Today is Oct 14, 2026. One year at a time, laid out like a calendar: a cell per month with that month's Safe to spend. The year's total sits on top.

Safe to spend is the same number as the Dashboard: income minus bills, savings and expenses. Past months use what happened; the current and future months use the plan, so they're marked EST. Tapping a month opens that month on the Dashboard.
