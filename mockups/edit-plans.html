Edit bills, savings and expected income from the Dashboard

Same white sheet as the expense editor: it slides up from the bottom on phones and in from the right on laptops. All numbers are placeholders. The month being viewed is Oct 2026.

Rule: amount changes ask “Just Oct 2026” or “Oct 2026 and later”. Months before Oct never change. Name, group and due day apply to every month, so they don't ask.

Closing: tapping the dimmed area outside a sheet closes it without saving, the same as ×. This applies to every sheet, including the existing expense sheet.
